# ORBCAFE UI Commercial License

ORBCAFE UI is source-available software. The [Community License](LICENSE)
is free for personal, educational, research, evaluation, development, and
internal business use. A **commercial license** is required when ORBCAFE UI
becomes part of a commercial software product or a paid customer delivery.

## When a commercial license is required

- **Commercial SaaS** — hosted or subscription services whose UI is built
  with ORBCAFE UI and offered to paying customers.
- **Commercial software products** — ERP, CRM, MES, WMS, AI/agent platforms,
  analytics platforms, developer platforms, and similar products that are
  sold, licensed, or commercially deployed and include ORBCAFE UI.
- **Paid customer delivery** — consulting, outsourcing, and
  system-integration projects delivered to a client for a fee, even when the
  resulting system is used only internally by that client.
- **OEM / white label** — embedding or rebranding ORBCAFE UI inside a
  product, platform, framework, or library provided to third parties.
- **Redistribution and resale** — selling or commercially redistributing
  ORBCAFE UI itself, modified or not, including as a component library, UI
  kit, design system, theme, or template.
- **Commercial templates** — paid admin/ERP/dashboard templates built with
  ORBCAFE UI.
- **Builder platforms** — low-code, no-code, UI builder, page builder,
  website builder, app builder, dashboard builder, or design-system
  platforms that expose ORBCAFE UI components to their customers.

Free community templates, demos, and teaching examples remain covered by the
Community License.

## What a commercial license can include

- Production usage rights for commercial products and SaaS
- Customer delivery rights for consulting and integration projects
- Redistribution, OEM, and white-label rights
- Enterprise support and upgrade rights

License tiers are planned as **Commercial Project**, **Enterprise**, and
**OEM**; terms are agreed per engagement while the program is being set up.
